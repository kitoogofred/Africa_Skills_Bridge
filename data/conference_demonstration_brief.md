# Africa Skills Bridge | V1.5.4 Conference Demonstration Brief

**Purpose:** Demonstrate an evidence-to-decision pathway for ICT–STI skills intelligence in Africa, aligned conceptually with the ILO STED/SPEED diagnostic stages 0–6.

**What is implemented:** World Bank descriptive labour-market context; live/recovered ESCO occupational taxonomy exploration; reproducible visual analysis (subject to current-run figure audit); evidence inventory; seven-stage diagnostic alignment; sector screening template; consultation and action registers.

**What is NOT established:** Observed Ugandan occupational demand or shortages; numbers of workers required; validated TVET supply gaps; sector prioritisation approved by stakeholders; completed ILO STED/SPEED diagnostic; SSEC/Council endorsement.

**Recommended five-minute flow:** 1) explain question and evidence sources; 2) show one World Bank comparison with date and caveat; 3) show the ESCO reference matrix; 4) show ILO stages and evidence gaps; 5) explain proposed stakeholder validation and skills response pathway.

**Next research inputs:** Sector economic and value-chain evidence; enterprise surveys/interviews; worker perspectives; occupation-level employment and forecasts; provider capacity and completion data; tracer studies; technical foresight; institutional validation and approvals.

**Interpretive caution:** ESCO is a European multilingual classification reference, not a measure of local demand. World Bank macro indicators are not occupation-specific shortages. Search-theme overlap is not employer-confirmed sector skills need. Synthetic data are demonstrations only.
